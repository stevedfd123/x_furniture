03 / OUR STORY

Rooted here.
Looking forward.

Anuradhapura, 08°20'N

X began with a simple question: what if the furniture around us felt as thoughtful as the lives lived upon it?

From our studio in Anuradhapura, we work with independent craftspeople and responsible suppliers to make contemporary furniture with a Sri Lankan point of view. Not trend-driven. Not disposable. Just considered objects that become part of your story.
